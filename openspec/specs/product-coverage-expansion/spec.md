# product-coverage-expansion Specification

## Purpose
Extend authored learning guidance, validated personal discovery storage and accessible lightweight discovery to every experiment, with traceable verification and conditional followup.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Catalog wide authored learning guidance
All140 experiments SHALL expose an authored exploration goal, searchable subject terms, reachable first-action link, a subject-specific prediction/transfer, prerequisites, view purpose and model/source boundaries. Existing four recommendations SHALL remain. Source-derived guidance MUST NOT imply external scientific or curriculum approval.

#### Scenario: Open any experiment
- **WHEN** a visitor opens any catalog entry at320px or390px
- **THEN** a first-action link reaches an actual relevant control and learning guidance remains readable with optional historical sources

### Requirement: Validated local discovery coverage
All140 experiments SHALL offer two explicitly saved personal discoveries, explanations, reload, recomputed restore and clear. Each experiment MUST validate its own numeric, enum or compound conditions, stop playback and clear independent credit on restore, without changing existing notebook behavior or fabricating unsaved trajectory history.

#### Scenario: Save an independent construction
- **WHEN** a visitor saves a circuit, puzzle or geometric construction then reloads and restores
- **THEN** its valid structure is restored and solved without awarding task completion

#### Scenario: Continuous controls and invalid snapshots
- **WHEN** a visitor saves a legal dragged value or encounters malformed stored conditions
- **THEN** legal continuous values survive and unknown fields, unsafe structures and incompatible versions are rejected

### Requirement: Lightweight complete directory
The complete directory SHALL retain140 readable links without JavaScript and support all authored goal filters while avoiding full scientific apparatus SVG rendering for every preview. Performance evidence MUST distinguish actual metrics and limitations.

#### Scenario: Read a lightweight directory
- **WHEN** a visitor searches or opens the no-script directory
- **THEN** the catalog remains complete and static preview graphics do not initialize models or replace actual experiment evidence

### Requirement: Executable verification and conditional followup
Delivery SHALL record Node24 verification where obtainable, all-route coverage, distinct review states, and a TODO for external dependencies with prerequisites and acceptance criteria. CI or publication MUST NOT be claimed executed without evidence.

#### Scenario: Inspect remaining work
- **WHEN** a maintainer reads delivery and TODO
- **THEN** executable work, actual passes, external verification and permission-dependent publication are separately traceable
