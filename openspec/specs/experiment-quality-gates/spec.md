# experiment-quality-gates Specification

## Purpose
Ensure every experiment offers historical context, scientifically meaningful manipulation and explanatory dual views, supported by individually traceable quality evidence.

## Requirements

### Requirement: History before investigation
Every experiment SHALL present meaningful historical context and a traceable reading source before or directly alongside the workbench. Principles or formulas alone MUST NOT constitute historical context.

#### Scenario: Start an experiment
- **WHEN** a student opens any experiment
- **THEN** they can read why the historical problem arose and find an explicit first action without scrolling past the complete workbench

### Requirement: Manipulation with observable consequences
Each experimental control MUST affect a scientifically appropriate observable or explicitly explain a genuine invariant. Dynamic phenomena SHALL provide state-linked playback and stepping, with time and conditions shared between two-dimensional and three-dimensional views.

#### Scenario: Operate a belt drive
- **WHEN** a student runs the drive or changes driven pulley radius
- **THEN** the belt and both pulleys move with consistent tangential speed and rotation ratio, readings update, and pause or step retains the same state in both views

### Requirement: Spatial information and explanation preserved
Three-dimensional views SHALL retain identification of essential parts and the evidence needed to understand the experiment. Physical apparatus MUST include relevant surfaces, depth and connections; planar mathematical evidence SHALL be identified honestly as planar constructions in space.

#### Scenario: Compare views
- **WHEN** a student switches or rotates the apparatus
- **THEN** the essential parts remain identifiable and linked to current measurements, rather than becoming an unlabeled outline with less teaching information

### Requirement: Individual quality audit
All 140 experiments MUST have an individually traceable audit covering history, first action, control response, dual-view meaning, and investigation. Failures SHALL be fixed or explicitly remain unresolved; test counts alone MUST NOT be represented as proof of overall product quality.

#### Scenario: Review quality evidence
- **WHEN** a maintainer reviews the repair delivery
- **THEN** the report lists individual experiment evidence and accurately states the depth and limits of verification
