# eighty-crafted-experiments Specification

## Purpose
Define eighty scientifically grounded additions across four learning spaces and require synchronized two-dimensional and three-dimensional representations, accessible investigation, and traceable verification for all 140 experiments.
## Requirements
### Requirement: Eighty distinct additions
The product SHALL add twenty distinct experiments to each of physics, geometry, cosmos and structures, identified P11–P30, M11–M30, C11–C30 and S11–S30, preserving all sixty existing routes and exposing 140 catalog entries.

#### Scenario: Browse a space
- **WHEN** a student opens a space or filters the catalog
- **THEN** all twenty additions are discoverable with accurate topic, stage and working route

### Requirement: Authored scientific and visual evidence
Every addition MUST have an independently specified model, subject-specific apparatus, at least two meaningful controlled scenes, current-state observations, units, derivation, historical context, source and declared limits. Undefined results MUST remain undefined; visual scaling MUST not change measurements.

#### Scenario: Change a condition
- **WHEN** a student edits an experimental variable or applies a scene
- **THEN** apparatus, readings, relevant evidence curves and observation explanation agree with one solved state

### Requirement: Independent and accessible investigation
All additions SHALL support original independent-task rules, undo/reset, two visual records, CSV, keyboard controls, 320/390px live observation and no-script reading. Scenes and restored records MUST NOT fabricate independent completion. Spatial additions MUST retain two-dimensional fallback after WebGL loss.

#### Scenario: Complete a comparison
- **WHEN** a student prepares, manually changes the specified variable and verifies two distinct conditions
- **THEN** independent evidence is recorded while changing unrelated conditions or applying scenes clears task eligibility

### Requirement: Individual verification before delivery
Delivery MUST include analytic or invariant model checks, all eighty browser task and scene checks, desktop/mobile screenshots with individual review evidence, old-route regressions and build/spec validation. Benchmarks and teaching validation MUST be described at the evidence depth actually achieved.

#### Scenario: Review delivery
- **WHEN** a maintainer reads the delivery report
- **THEN** every addition has traceable design, scientific checks, operation and visual evidence, with unresolved limitations stated honestly

### Requirement: Complete synchronized dual-view coverage
All 140 experiments MUST expose interactive two-dimensional and rotatable three-dimensional representations linked to the same live state and data. Existing experiments missing either representation SHALL be completed without breaking their established controls, tasks or evidence. Planar constructions in three-dimensional space MUST use actual geometry rather than screenshot textures.

#### Scenario: Compare existing and new views
- **WHEN** a student changes parameters or apparatus state in any experiment and opens its three-dimensional view
- **THEN** both representations reflect the current state and measurements, and failure to create WebGL retains a usable two-dimensional experiment
