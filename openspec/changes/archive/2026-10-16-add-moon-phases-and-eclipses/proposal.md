## Why
The next content-roadmap work is moon phases and eclipses. Visitors need a convincing sky view paired with manipulable spatial geometry to distinguish ordinary phases from Earth shadows and explain why eclipses do not occur every month.

## What Changes
- Add an original two-view observatory at `/labs/moon/`, with textured 3D Earth/Moon, direct orbital manipulation and same-state 2D geometry.
- Derive phase, angular size, local solar occultation and lunar shadow evidence from physical radius/distance ratios; distinguish compressed display scale from physical calculation.
- Provide standard phase/eclipse demonstrations, prediction, eclipse-alignment challenge, live formulas and a lamp-and-ball activity.
- Expand the catalog to twelve experiments and complete requirements/design/interfaces/operation/validation documentation in `doc/`.
- After delivery, review recent labs together and implement bounded shared experience improvements and record remaining priorities for the next iteration.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `moon-phases-and-eclipses`: physically explained, realistic dual-view moon phases and eclipse exploration with accessible fallback and guided learning.
### Modified Capabilities
- `spatial-lab-presentation`: compact same-model mobile evidence and return-to-stage navigation for the recent spatial labs.

## Impact
New pure astronomy model/tests, Three.js scene, DOM/Canvas/SVG controller, page and styles. Catalog, cosmos exploration trail, browser acceptance scripts and local NASA-derived texture assets. No backend or new runtime dependency. Existing lab review follows new-content delivery.
