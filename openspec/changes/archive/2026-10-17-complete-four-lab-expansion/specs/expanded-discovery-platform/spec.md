## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Forty complete original experiments
The catalog SHALL retain the existing20 experiments and add exactly10 per space, with60 real working routes and17/17/13/13 counts. The structures URL SHALL remain while its name becomes工程与建造实验室. Each space MUST expose meaningful topic groups and original experiment illustrations/history.

#### Scenario: Browse complete catalog
- **WHEN** a student enters any of the four spaces
- **THEN** all experiments are accessible under relevant topics without placeholder routes or fabricated completion

### Requirement: Accessible reproducible workbenches
Every new experiment MUST provide validated free inputs, constrained dependent geometry, keyboard/native controls, undo/redo/reset, appropriate playback and two current-page records with versioned unit-bearing CSV. Restore/reference/undo MUST clear task credit and unsaved traces. Pointer/touch interaction MUST have44px targets and equivalent native controls.

#### Scenario: Restore observation
- **WHEN** a saved record is restored
- **THEN** model observations recompute and task success/unsaved history are not forged

### Requirement: Honest scientific explanation
Every experiment SHALL include known conditions, independent first task, counterexample, reasoning, provenance and topic-level prerequisites. Cosmos MUST distinguish verified physics, approximations, engineering proposals and novel assumptions. Synthetic data, exaggerated scales and model validity limits MUST be visible.

#### Scenario: Cross a model boundary
- **WHEN** inputs invalidate a model or reach a singular/degenerate state
- **THEN** the interface explains the boundary and does not report a valid result or independent success

### Requirement: Resilient responsive visualization
New experiments SHALL render readable static no-script diagrams and disabled interactive controls. Spatial experiments MUST provide3D and usable2D evidence without WebGL. Layouts MUST fit320/390px, respect reduced motion and pause on backgrounding; shaders and textures MUST not block first operation.

#### Scenario: No WebGL or no JavaScript
- **WHEN** the graphical runtime is unavailable
- **THEN** students can read conditions/model/history and, with JavaScript, operate the native2D evidence controls
