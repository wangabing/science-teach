## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Arc-dependent inscribed angles
The studio MUST derive the actual angle APB, show same-arc invariance and opposite-arc supplementary angles, and treat P=A or P=B as undefined.

#### Scenario: Same chord opposite arcs
- **WHEN** A is at0 degrees, B at120 degrees, and P moves from240 to60 degrees
- **THEN** the inscribed angle changes from60 to120 degrees and the intercepted arc changes accordingly

### Requirement: Two-position observation
A guided observation SHALL require two manually reached nondegenerate positions on the same arc under fixed chord conditions and provide a separate reasoning route using radii.

#### Scenario: Endpoint
- **WHEN** P reaches a chord endpoint
- **THEN** no angle or task completion is reported, and the student is prompted to move off the endpoint
