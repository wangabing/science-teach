## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Whole catalog experience risk screening
The maintainer SHALL run one reproducible screening over all catalog routes and receive individual findings for entry, browser errors, primary apparatus response, measurements and spatial operation. Changed captions alone MUST NOT establish apparatus response.

#### Scenario: Numbers change without visible scientific evidence
- **WHEN** a parameter changes measurements but leaves the non-text apparatus and scientifically justified measurement evidence unchanged
- **THEN** the report retains a visible-response risk with the parameter and before/after evidence rather than marking teaching quality passed

### Requirement: Explicit task and review status
Each route SHALL have a learning task and separate automated, student-task and teaching-review states. External student and teacher review states MUST remain explicitly deferred until post-launch feedback; they MUST NOT be reported as completed or block product implementation acceptance. Findings SHALL distinguish functional blockers, insufficient phenomena, interaction problems and presentation improvements.

#### Scenario: Complete automatic screening
- **WHEN** all pages have been checked automatically
- **THEN** the report still identifies outstanding manual reviews and unresolved findings with reproducible commands

### Requirement: Product defect closure independent of external review
The product SHALL retain baseline findings and their resolutions, verify meaningful process states, and provide visible scientific evidence for changing measurements. External student and teacher reviews SHALL be deferred to post-launch feedback and SHALL NOT block this implementation acceptance.

#### Scenario: A physical invariant is encountered
- **WHEN** a parameter changes forces or time scale while the fixed geometry remains invariant
- **THEN** the experiment explains that invariant and displays the corresponding force, calibrated measurement or process response without inventing deformation
