## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Individually authored experiment journeys
All forty new experiments SHALL provide a subject-specific first action, meaningful primary controls, at least two validated exploratory scenes, observation prompts and a legend. Scene application MUST clear independent task credit and remain undoable.

#### Scenario: Explore a scene then return to independent work
- **WHEN** a student applies a named scene and prepares an independent task
- **THEN** inputs reflect the scene with no fabricated credit and preparation restores the original task conditions

### Requirement: Legible instruments and spatial evidence
Every experiment SHALL provide a polished subject-specific apparatus or construction linked to current model inputs and observations. Three-dimensional lines and rods MUST remain readable and retain the two-dimensional fallback. Presentation MUST not invent physical values or silently change geometric constraints.

#### Scenario: Change an experimental condition
- **WHEN** a student edits a primary control
- **THEN** the apparatus, highlighted observation and relevant chart marker update from the same solved state

### Requirement: Focused accessible controls and mobile observation
The workbench SHALL prioritize first-use controls and disclose advanced parameters on demand. At320/390px a live apparatus preview MUST accompany parameter operation without blocking controls, with keyboard alternatives and reduced-motion support. Static no-script diagrams and disabled controls MUST remain available.

#### Scenario: Operate on a small screen
- **WHEN** a student scrolls to adjust a parameter
- **THEN** a compact current-state apparatus is available beside the tools and the page has no horizontal overflow

### Requirement: Per-experiment quality evidence
Delivery MUST include an individual review record for all forty experiments, desktop/mobile captures, independent task and scene checks, model regressions and resource/fallback verification. Benchmark research MUST distinguish pages actually read from prior research and inaccessible sources.

#### Scenario: Review completed polish
- **WHEN** a maintainer opens the delivery record
- **THEN** all forty experiments have named improvements and reproducible verification evidence without claiming real classroom validation
