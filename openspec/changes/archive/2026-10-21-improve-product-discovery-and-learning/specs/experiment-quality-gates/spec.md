## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Pilot first action within reach
The six health-review pilots SHALL show an explicit first-action entry within the initial 390x844 viewport and allow a single activation to reach the relevant apparatus or original task control. Historical context SHALL remain before or alongside the workbench with readable expanded sources. Keyboard, 320px layout, reduced motion and 2D fallback SHALL remain available.

#### Scenario: Begin on a phone
- **WHEN** a visitor opens a pilot and activates its first-action entry
- **THEN** the relevant usable control or apparatus is reached without a demonstration completing independent work
